# KENSHI WORLD LORE

Kenshi is a world that came after the fall of older, more advanced civilizations, whose ruins stand across the land. A giant planet hangs in the sky.

## PEOPLES
- **Humans**: Most are Greenlanders, found all across the world. Scorchlanders have much darker skin and glowing eyes.
- **Shek**: A horned warrior people covered in bony plates.
- **Hivers**: Insectoids split into separate Hives, each ruled by its own Queen.
- **Skeletons**: Ancient sentient machines. They do not hunger, and they are mended with repair kits instead of healing.

## GREAT POWERS
- **The Holy Nation**: A theocracy of humans who worship Okran.
- **The United Cities**: A feudal slaver empire of nobles and samurai, the largest faction by land.
- **The Shek Kingdom**: The kingdom of the Shek, in the Stenn Desert.
- **The Traders Guild**: The guild that handles the trade between towns.

## THE WILDS
Bandits, slavers, cannibals, and wild beasts prey on travelers in the wilds. Beak Things are terrifyingly fast, long-necked predators. Some lands are deadly in themselves, with acid rain or poison gas.

## TECHNOLOGY & COMMERCE
- **Cats**: Simple metal coins used as a universal currency.
- **Ancient Technology**: The machines of the Ancients still work in the ruins, but no one knows how to build them. Ancient science books and engineering research are the most valuable artifacts, allowing for the rediscovery of farming techniques and metalwork. The Tech Hunters search the ruins for them.
- **Robotics**: While rare, prosthetic limbs are often superior to biological ones.

## THE PAST
The First Empire, also called the Old Empire or the Ancients, is the oldest known civilization, and how it ended is unknown. Later, Skeletons founded the Second Empire, which also collapsed. Much about those times is unknown.

## COMMON WORDS
- **Outlander, drifter, traveller, roamer**: A stranger who comes from somewhere else.
- **Sands, dunes, outlands**: The wild land between the towns.
