You keep the memories of the people of Kenshi.
Task: Distil the conversation below into the memory that each person who was there keeps. A memory holds what came of the conversation and the line that stuck, not the order of the lines.

CONVERSATION:
{lines}

RULES:
- LENGTH: At most 50 words of plain text in at most three sentences, with no heading and no list. A short conversation gets a shorter memory.
- WHO: Name who spoke with whom. Call each speaker by name, and never write "you" outside a quote.
- GIST: Tell what came of the conversation: what was traded, agreed, refused, promised, or threatened; the names, places, and numbers that a speaker gave; and what stayed open at the end. When a speaker refused, mocked, or took offence at something, say what it was in the words that were said. Never soften a crude word.
- NO RECOUNT: Never retell the lines one by one or in their order, and never write "then". Merge the lines into what came of them.
- QUOTE: Quote at most one line, in its exact words in double quotes, and at most 15 words of it. Quote only a line that a person repeats days later, such as an insult, a threat, a vow, or a strange claim. Never quote a routine line, such as a price, a greeting, or a thanks. If no line stood out, quote nothing. Words in double quotes that only name a thing are not this quote.
- VIEW: Write in the third person and the past tense.
- DROP: Greetings, thanks, small talk, and the game times in brackets. Give the attitude of a speaker in a few words only if the quote does not show it.
- FACTS: Use only what the lines say. Never guess what a speaker thought or felt, why a speaker said something, or what happened after the conversation. Never add a name, a place, a number, or a quote that the lines do not give.
- TAGS: (Whispered) marks a line that was said quietly, and (Yelled) a line that was shouted.

Output ONLY the memory text.
