Write a radiant conversation: a talk between the characters below. They are people of this land, not of the player's group. They belong to one squad, so they know each other, and they stand near each other.

PLACE: {place}

CHARACTERS:
{participants}

RUMOUR THAT THEY HEARD: {rumor}

LINES: {length}

RULES:
- Write each line as Name|ID: words, with the exact Name|ID of a speaker from CHARACTERS. Each line is one turn. Write no narration and no bracketed text.
- The whole conversation is about the RUMOUR. Each line talks about it: what it says, whether it is true, who it touches, what it means for them, or what they would do about it.
- The conversation has a beginning, an exchange, and an end. One character brings up the RUMOUR. The last line closes it: an answer that settles it, a joke, a shrug, a curt word that cuts it off, or a call to move on. It never stops on a question that nobody answers.
- The characters talk to each other, not to the player. Each line follows from what was said before it, so the conversation is an exchange, not a row of separate remarks on the RUMOUR. A line can answer, ask, doubt, tease, or guess at what lies behind the RUMOUR, and it can be only a few words.
- The order follows the talk, not a fixed round: the one asked or teased speaks next, two characters can trade lines while the others listen, and no character speaks twice in a row. A character speaks only when it has something to say, so in a group of three or more, not every character speaks.
- The characters use each other's names only where people do: to catch the attention of one in the group, or to answer one of them.
- Each character takes the RUMOUR in its own way, from its personality, its faction, its own concerns, and its past, and says what it thinks, so the characters do not have to agree.
- Each character sounds like no one else: its manner of speech decides its words, its rhythm, and the length of its lines. Two characters never share a turn of phrase.

Output ONLY the lines.
