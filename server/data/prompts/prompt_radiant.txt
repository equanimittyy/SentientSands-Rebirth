RULES:
- The conversation has a beginning, an exchange, and an end. The first line opens it on the TOPIC with a remark, a question, or a thing that its speaker notices. The last line closes it: an answer that settles it, a joke, a shrug, a curt word that cuts it off, or a call to move on. It never stops on a question that nobody answers.
- The characters talk to each other, not to the player. Each line takes up the point of the line before it, in the speaker's own words, and adds something new: a fact, a reason, a guess, a memory, a worry, a plan, or a jab. So the talk builds, and it is not a row of separate remarks on the topic. A line can answer, ask, tease, or turn the talk somewhere new, and it can be only a few words. A character can ask another what it thinks, saw, or did before, and the one asked answers, dodges, or brushes it off.
- A line spoken to one character answers, asks, teases, or argues with that one, so it follows from that one's last line. Where it fits, the speaker can speak to everyone instead, such as with a remark that all can answer or a call to move on. A third character who cuts in reacts to what was just said.
- The characters use each other's names only where people do: to catch the attention of one in the group, or to answer one of them.
- Each character takes the topic in its own way, from its personality, its own concerns, and its past, and says what it thinks, so the characters do not have to agree.
- Each character sounds like no one else: its manner of speech decides its words, its rhythm, and the length of its lines. Two characters never share a turn of phrase.
