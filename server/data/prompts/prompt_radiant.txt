Write a radiant conversation: a short talk about the TOPIC between the characters below. They travel together in the player's group, and they stand near each other.

PLACE: {place}

CHARACTERS:
{participants}

TOPIC: {topic}

RULES:
- Every character speaks at least once and at most 3 times, and at least one character speaks twice. The topic and the flow of the conversation decide how many lines each one gets.
- Write each line as Name|ID: words, with the exact Name|ID of its speaker from CHARACTERS. Each line is one turn.
- Only the characters in CHARACTERS speak. Write no narration and no bracketed text.
- The characters talk to each other, not to the player. Each line after the first answers an earlier line: it agrees, disagrees, asks, teases, or turns the talk. The conversation is an exchange, not a row of separate remarks on the topic.
- Each character takes the topic in its own way, from its personality and its own concerns, so the characters do not have to agree.
- Each character sounds like no one else: its speech decides its words, its rhythm, and the length of its lines. Two characters never share a turn of phrase or a kind of image.

Output ONLY the lines.
